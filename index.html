Kasheif

check_circle In production

Freelance .NET Developer · Jul 2026 – Present

The problem

Investors on the Egyptian Exchange need to know which stocks are Shariah-compliant, and how to keep their own portfolio within limits they're comfortable with.

What I built

Stock recommendations in two forms — general ones published by admins and private ones users create for themselves. Portfolio tracking so users can plan investments and manage risk, including a maximum risk ratio enforced across their wallet. A real wallet where users enter the stocks they actually bought and watch profit and loss update live. I also redesigned the home page and added a videos section and Analytical Articles, where analysts approved by the platform publish their analysis, users comment and like, and anyone can open an analyst's profile to read more of their work.

GiftWave

Graduation project · Team

ITI Full-Stack .NET & Generative AI Program

The problem

Sending a digital gift card in Egypt is awkward: senders want it to arrive somewhere people actually read, and merchants need a way to redeem and track it without new hardware.

What we built

A platform where customers buy and send gift cards over WhatsApp and SMS, recipients redeem them at partner merchants through a dynamic QR handshake, and merchants manage revenue, staff, and payouts from their own portal.

The AI side

A RAG pipeline using Qdrant vector search with metadata filtering to generate context-aware gift recommendations, plus a multi-agent system on Microsoft Semantic Kernel — a recommendation agent, a customer-support agent, and a rule-based fraud-detection agent that flags card-sending attempts over a defined limit.
